How recommendations work

We compare your selected interests, region and budget against a small curated destination library. Costs are illustrative USD estimates using a base trip assumption per traveler plus accommodation and daily spending. Departure location affects the planning allowance, but the tool does not retrieve live airfares. Destinations and estimated costs require independent verification before booking.

Affiliate links, when activated, may earn us a commission at no additional cost to you. Sponsorship never changes the math or hides alternative destinations.
