Ready to book?

Each idea includes an affiliate link to Expedia. We may earn a commission if you book through it. The link opens Expedia; enter your destination, dates and travelers there. Your planner selections are not sent to Expedia. Availability, prices and bookings are handled by Expedia. You can also compare directly with hotels or other booking providers.

How recommendations work

We compare your selected interests, region and budget against a small curated destination library. Costs are illustrative USD estimates using a base trip assumption per traveler plus accommodation and daily spending. Departure location affects the planning allowance, but the tool does not retrieve live airfares. Destinations and estimated costs require independent verification before booking.

Our Expedia affiliate links may earn us a commission. The current destination comparison uses the same illustrative cost formula for every listed destination; the link does not establish that Expedia offers the lowest price.
