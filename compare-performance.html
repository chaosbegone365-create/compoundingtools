How to prepare your files

Export historical portfolio values from your brokerage or spreadsheet, with a date and a total value on each row. Select a benchmark above; no ETF file is required. Your portfolio file is processed locally in your browser.

Important: A portfolio balance series with deposits, withdrawals, or transfers is not a valid investment return series. This initial tool cannot calculate time-weighted or money-weighted returns. We plan to add transaction imports and cash-flow adjustment later.
