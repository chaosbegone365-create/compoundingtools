# Compound Your Future — growth and sponsorship plan

Domain: compoundyourfuture.com. March 2027 goal: $2,000/month gross revenue (stretch goal, not forecast). Keep infrastructure within free-tier limits; do not promise fully autonomous publishing or earnings.

## Positioning
Free, privacy-conscious, mobile-first calculators that show assumptions, formulas, examples, and practical next steps. Avoid personalized investment recommendations.

## SEO launch sequence
1. Verify production deployment, HTTPS, canonical domain, and indexability. Configure Search Console and submit an XML sitemap.
2. Build distinct, useful pages for compound interest, retirement savings, Coast FIRE, dividend income, savings goals, and inflation-adjusted growth. Each needs a descriptive title, unique explanatory copy, formula, worked example, FAQs, and accessible interactive controls.
3. Use logical internal links, breadcrumbs, structured data only where eligible, clean URLs, and mobile performance checks. Do not mass-publish thin AI articles.
4. Publish 2–3 carefully reviewed articles weekly targeting specific problems and linking to the corresponding calculators. Include update dates and reputable sources.
5. Measure indexed pages, impressions, organic clicks, engaged calculator sessions, affiliate conversions, and revenue weekly. Improve pages with impressions but weak click-through rates.

## Monetization
- Apply to contextual advertising only after the site has substantive original content and required policy pages; approval is not assured.
- Apply to relevant financial affiliate programs, with conspicuous disclosure and careful vetting of claims.
- Offer founding sponsor placements as a clearly labeled fixed-term pilot; never imply traffic or guaranteed leads that do not exist.

## Founding sponsor pilot
Offer a 60-day founding partner package: one clearly labeled placement on a relevant calculator, one optional sponsored educational resource subject to editorial review, and an end-of-pilot report. Initial asking price can be tested at $100–$250/month per partner, but is hypothetical and may be worth $0 before traffic. Avoid exclusivity, guaranteed conversions, and selling user data.

Prospect categories: budgeting apps, financial education services, tax software, reputable investment research providers, and financial planning tools. Exclude predatory loans and unsuitable speculative promotions. Verify contact addresses and sponsor fit before outreach; send individually tailored, compliant messages, not bulk spam.

## Revenue scenarios (illustrative, not predictions)
At $15 revenue per 1,000 pageviews, ads alone would require about 133,334 monthly pageviews for $2,000. A mix of 4 sponsors at $200/month, $500/month affiliate commissions, and $700/month ads would total $2,000, but every component depends on traction and agreements.

## Execution gates
Week 1: audit existing calculators, rebrand, confirm hosting, domain, metadata, analytics, sitemap, privacy and disclosure pages.
Weeks 2–4: publish 5–6 tested cornerstone calculators and 8–10 original supporting articles; prepare media kit and prospect list.
Months 2–3: targeted outreach, technical SEO refinements, partnerships, and measured conversion experiments.
Months 4–5: double down on pages and partnerships with demonstrated results. Review economics and revise the March target if evidence does not support it.

Owner approvals required for account setup, domain DNS, contractual commitments, and sending external sponsor emails. Never claim outreach has been sent until confirmed.
