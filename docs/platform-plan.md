# CompoundingTools — Platform Product Plan
Last revised: 2026-10-25
Status: public beta; not approved for broad promotion.

## Strategic objective
Build a human, attractive, useful, trustworthy and largely automated decision-support website. Stretch target: $2,000 monthly gross revenue by March 2027. Target recurring software budget: $0 within free-tier limits. No promise of traffic or earnings.

## Product divisions and launch sequence
| Division | First tools | Later tools | Data / risk notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Investing & Options | Compound growth; long call/put expiry P&L | Covered calls, puts, spreads, Black–Scholes, implied volatility, dividend and tax scenarios | Distinguish expiration payoffs from pre-expiry valuation; educational only |
| Compensation & Benefits | Bonus calculator | Am I Underpaid, 401(k) match, deferred compensation, RSUs, job offers | BLS and licensed wage data; complex tax/plan rules |
| Retirement & FIRE | Savings projection | Coast FIRE, Barista FIRE, one-income retirement, withdrawal stress tests | Inflation, sequence risk and tax assumptions |
| Family & Life | One-income family | Childcare, parental leave, career breaks, college savings | Household tax and benefit complexity |
| Wealth & Identity | Wealth percentile | Geographic comparisons, hidden head start, college cohorts | Federal Reserve and Census; avoid unsupported rankings |
| Travel | Vacation budget | Destination finder, itineraries, points, creator links | Clearly separate estimated and live prices; creator attribution |
| Housing & Consumer | Mortgage | Rent vs buy, property taxes, cars, renovations | Geographic and time-sensitive data |
| Health & Fitness | Basic calorie estimate | Protein, activity, sleep, fitness costs | Health tools need heightened review; no peptide self-prescribing advice |
| Education | College cost comparison | Degree ROI, school comparisons, graduate school | College Scorecard data limitations |
| Side Income | Hourly side-gig earnings | Creator, barre, tutoring, freelance business | Earnings are scenarios, not forecasts |

## Site information architecture
Homepage: high-quality hero, search, featured tools, visual category tiles, credibility section, suggestions.
Category pages: distinct introductory content, relevant tools, methodology and related questions.
Tool pages: unique URL, user inputs, clear results, assumptions, accessibility, related tools, source references, disclaimer.
Trust: About, Privacy, Terms, Methodology, contact/suggestion pathway.
Business: premium digital product catalog and automatic delivery only after checkout integration is authorized.

## Delivery waves
Wave 0 (current): existing calculators, options expiry calculator, visual categories, trust pages, beta noindex.
Wave 1: calculator regression tests, live deployment verification, improved imagery and mobile QA, standalone calculator URLs, accessible suggestion form, privacy review.
Wave 2: Coast FIRE, one-income household, options spread tool, job offer comparison, vacation budget prototype.
Wave 3: data-backed salary and wealth comparison products with verified data rights, premium spreadsheet downloads.
Wave 4: partner widgets, expanded travel discovery, SEO and conversion experiments, optional subscriptions after evidence of demand.

## Editorial and safety gates
- All published calculations need formula examples and regression tests.
- All source-backed claims need citation, vintage, and reuse permission.
- High-stakes medical and financial material requires appropriate safeguards.
- No fabricated comparisons, salary percentiles, returns, prices, or earnings.
- No scaled near-duplicate SEO pages.
- Do not remove noindex until quality, privacy, and functional checks pass.

## Autonomous backlog
Target 400 vetted opportunities. Once <=100 viable unstarted opportunities remain, generate 500 new candidates, deduplicate and validate before adding. This is a desired operating policy, not an active automation until implemented and verified.

## Marketing
SEO: unique helpful pages, internal links, accessible UX, clear methodology, Search Console and analytics when connected.
Retention: related tools, optional browser-local favorites, return-worthy updates.
Feedback: replace GitHub-account-required issue link with accessible, spam-protected submission.
Monetization: free tools, optional $19–$99 premium products, ads when eligible, future B2B licensing.
Metrics: search impressions, visits, completions, shares, product views, conversion, gross revenue, fees, refunds, operating margin, failed checks.

## Critical blockers
Custom .com requires domain registration and DNS ownership; free GitHub Pages URL remains the working address.
Search Console and checkout require separate authorization.
Live deploy, mobile QA, and calculations require independent verification.
