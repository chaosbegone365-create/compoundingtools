How our calculations work

This page explains our core financial formulas. Each calculator also describes its own inputs, assumptions and limits beside the tool so you can check the scenario independently.

Bonus and compensation

Gross bonus = annual base salary × target bonus rate × payout percentage. Gross annual cash compensation = base salary + calculated bonus. Taxes, deductions, and other awards are excluded.

Investment growth

Each month: ending balance = prior balance × (1 + annual nominal return ÷ 1200) + monthly contribution. Contributions are assumed to occur at month-end. This is a deterministic illustration, not a market forecast.

Retirement savings

The investment growth formula is applied from current age to retirement age. The nominal future balance is discounted to today's dollars by dividing by (1 + annual inflation rate) raised to the number of years. Taxes, fees, and withdrawals are excluded.

Scenario tools, not personal recommendations

Prefilled values are illustrations unless a tool identifies a dated source. A lower modeled cost does not establish suitability. Travel budgets are not live availability or bookable quotes; workout schedules and meal plans are educational examples, not individualized health advice. School-cost comparisons do not predict admissions or financial aid. Cash-goal models do not choose an investment allocation or account for you.

Where a tool supports downloads, files may contain your inputs. Page-link sharing excludes those inputs. Review the specific tool’s explanation before sharing or acting on an estimate.

Important limitations

Actual returns vary, and negative outcomes are possible. Inputs are not independently verified. Results should be considered scenarios rather than predictions.
