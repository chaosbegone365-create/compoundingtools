Mortgage payment estimator

Estimate the principal-and-interest payment for a fixed-rate mortgage. Use this tool to compare loan amounts, interest rates and repayment terms—not to determine how much home you can afford.

Results update as you edit and are rounded to the nearest dollar. This is principal and interest only: property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance (including PMI), HOA charges, closing costs, lender fees and maintenance are not included. The example rate is illustrative, not a quote.

How to use this estimate

  1. Enter the amount you expect to borrow, rather than the purchase price. If the price is $450,000 and the down payment is $100,000, enter $350,000.
  2. Enter a hypothetical or quoted annual interest rate. Use the loan's interest rate, not its APR, because APR incorporates additional borrowing costs.
  3. Enter the repayment term in years. Compare scenarios by changing one input at a time; the result updates without a new page load.

For the same loan and positive interest rate, a longer term generally lowers the monthly principal-and-interest payment but increases total interest if you keep the loan for its full term. This tool does not compare fees or decide which loan is suitable for you.

Worked example

For a $350,000 loan at an illustrative 6.5% annual interest rate over 30 years, the monthly principal-and-interest payment is approximately $2,212, rounded to the nearest dollar.

The unrounded calculation is $2,212.238082 per month across 360 monthly periods. A zero-interest $120,000 loan over 10 years would instead require $1,000 per month.

These examples exclude all other ownership and borrowing costs. Actual lender schedules may differ because payments are rounded to cents and terms can vary.

Methodology and assumptions

Let P be the loan amount, r the annual interest-rate percentage entered divided by 1,200, and n the term in years multiplied by 12. Here r is the monthly interest rate—not the mortgage APR.

For a positive interest rate, monthly payment = P × r ÷ [1 − (1 + r)−n]. At a zero interest rate, monthly payment = P ÷ n.

The model assumes a constant interest rate, regular monthly payments at the end of each period and full repayment over the entered term. It uses a numerically stable equivalent of this formula for very small rates. It does not model extra payments, adjustable rates, interest-only periods, balloon payments, arrears or refinancing.

Your total housing payment will be different

This result is not an affordability assessment or an estimate of cash needed at closing. Add property taxes, insurance and applicable mortgage insurance to understand the broader monthly obligation. HOA charges and maintenance may be separate from the payment to your lender.

For an actual offer, compare the lender's Loan Estimate, including the projected total monthly payment and closing costs. Do not treat this simplified result as a lender quote or approval.

Inputs must be present, finite and nonnegative; the term must be greater than zero. If a value is unsupported, the tool replaces the estimate with an error message instead of retaining a stale result.
