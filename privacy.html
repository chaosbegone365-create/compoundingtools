Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026.

The current Compound Your Future calculators run directly in your browser. The website does not currently provide user accounts or a server-side calculator submission system. Calculator inputs are not intentionally transmitted to Compound Your Future servers.

GitHub Pages and underlying network providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, for delivery and security. External fonts or images may also result in requests to third-party providers.

The website’s feedback link opens your email app to send a message to compoundyourfuture@yahoo.com. Your email address, message and any attachments are processed by your email provider and Yahoo under their policies. Do not include sensitive personal information. If you choose to use the repository’s GitHub Issues channel instead, submissions are handled by GitHub and may be publicly visible.

External services and affiliate links

This release retains Google AdSense ownership metadata but does not load the AdSense advertising script. This does not establish account approval or verification status. Do not interpret browser-based calculations as a promise that no third party receives technical information: map tiles, external fonts and other third-party resources can receive request information. See Google’s information about sites that use its services.

Clearly labeled affiliate links take you to independently operated providers. We may earn a commission from qualifying purchases. Those providers handle their own cookies, tracking, bookings, accounts and payments under their policies. We do not process payments on this website. Do not send passwords, account numbers, payment details or child identifiers through this website or public GitHub Issues.

Copying or downloading a schedule, shopping list or scenario can include the information you entered. Keep those files private as appropriate. A page-link share control, where available, shares the page address only—not calculator inputs or results. Your chosen sharing service applies its own policies.

For project-related privacy questions, email compoundyourfuture@yahoo.com without including confidential information.
