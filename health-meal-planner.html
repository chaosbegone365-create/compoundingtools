Cooking and food safety

These original recipes are planning starting points, not kitchen-tested guarantees. Portion needs and cooking times vary. Use a food thermometer: poultry, including ground turkey, must reach 165°F (74°C); salmon 145°F (63°C); egg dishes 160°F (71°C). Keep raw meat and fish separate from ready-to-eat food and clean hands, surfaces and utensils after handling them.

Refrigerate or freeze perishable food within two hours, or within one hour above 90°F (32°C). Keep the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). A seven-day plan is not an instruction to store all cooked meals in the refrigerator for a week; plan safe storage or freeze portions.
